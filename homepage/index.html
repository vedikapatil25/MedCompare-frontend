Quick Health Tips

Simple habits to keep you healthy every day

Take vitamins with food

Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) absorb best when taken with a meal containing healthy fats.

Store medicines correctly

Keep medicines away from heat and humidity. Avoid storing them in bathrooms or near windows.

Always check expiry dates

Expired medicines can be ineffective or harmful. Always verify before purchasing or consuming.

Stay hydrated daily

Drink at least 8 glasses of water a day. Proper hydration helps medicines work more effectively.

Complete your course

Always finish your full antibiotic course even if you feel better early, to prevent resistance.

Consult before switching

Never switch to a generic or alternate medicine without consulting your doctor or pharmacist first.
